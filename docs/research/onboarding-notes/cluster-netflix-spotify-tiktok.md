# Onboarding flows: Netflix, Spotify, TikTok (as of 2026-10-01)

Raw note from a research sub-agent of the first (stalled) inventory run on 2026-10-01, kept because its findings are dated and sourced. Method caveats from the agent: its web search budget ran out partway; TikTok's help pages rendered as empty shells; Mobbin and two Medium teardowns returned 403; one Netflix how-to sat behind a paywall; web.archive.org could not be fetched.

## Netflix (a new profile)

- Flow: (1) from an existing account, open Manage Profiles (TV: Add Profile; web: Manage Profiles; app: My Netflix, profile name, Manage Profiles); (2) if the owner opted into "Require PIN to add new profiles" (off by default), enter the PIN; (3) Add Profile; (4) a name (free text) and an icon from a preset grid; (5) a Kids toggle, the same flow for kids and adults; (6) Continue; (7) on first use of the profile, Netflix asks the user to pick a few titles they like to jump-start recommendations, explicitly skippable; (8) Home. Maturity ratings, viewing restrictions, and autoplay are set afterwards under Edit Profile.
- Format: the title step is widely described as a browsable grid of title tiles (teardown), not a search box; not confirmed by a 2026 screenshot (medium confidence).
- Minimum items required: none per Netflix's current wording ("a few titles", optional, no number). A teardown describes the new-account equivalent as picking "3 shows", a figure widely repeated, with no primary source for an enforced minimum.
- Skippable: yes, explicitly; skippers get "a diverse and popular set of titles". The only non-skippable gate is the optional PIN.
- Imports: none in the profile flow.
- Asked later: viewing restrictions and playback settings in Edit Profile; explicit ratings moved from five stars to thumbs in 2017 (TechCrunch, 2017-03-16); viewing behavior supersedes the initial picks once the profile is used.
- Screens before first useful result: about 3 to 4 (medium confidence; inferred from Netflix's help wording plus new-account teardowns).
- Company statement: the Help Center frames the picker as a way to jump start recommendations, optional, with a popular-titles fallback, and says real viewing takes over: a cold-start bridge, not a lasting input. No metrics.
- Sources: company statements, Netflix Help Center, retrieved 2026-10-01: [How Netflix's Recommendations System Works](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/100639), [How to create, change, or delete profiles](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/10421), [How to create a profile for kids](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/114275), [How to require a PIN to add a new profile](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/122551); teardown, [UserOnboarding.Academy on Netflix signup](https://useronboarding.academy/user-onboarding-inspirations/netflix-signup-onboarding), undated; press, [TechCrunch, 2017-03-16](https://techcrunch.com/2017/03/16/netflix-is-replacing-five-star-ratings-with-thumbs-up-or-down/).
- Gaps: no 2026 screenshot teardown of the new-profile case; no confirmed minimum; grid versus search inferred; TheStreamable how-to paywalled (402).

## Spotify

- Flow, iOS (Pageflows capture June 2022, stable back to April 2018): start screen; email; password; date of birth; gender; name; notification permission; artist selection screens; podcast selection screens with a search box beside the grid; a confirmation message; setup complete; the App Tracking Transparency prompt; Home. Flow, web (Pageflows capture March 2024): homepage, sign up, email, password, one combined name, birthday, and gender step, terms, Home, with no artist, genre, or podcast picker at all.
- Format: a pre-populated tile grid of artist and podcast photos with a search box alongside, not genre chips.
- Minimum items required: secondary sources converge on at least three artists before continuing, iOS only; not confirmed from a primary screenshot (the Medium post returned 403). Moot on web.
- Skippable: email, password, date of birth, and name mandatory; gender and notifications reported skippable; whether the artist picker has a Skip affordance is unconfirmed.
- Imports: none inside the sign-up wizard. Facebook friend-finding is a later Settings action; Spotify Community threads report Facebook login no longer offered as of 2026 (user-report confidence).
- Asked later: friend-following from Settings; a Taste Profile beta (newsroom, 2026-03-13) lets active users ask for more or less of a vibe or for unheard artists, framed for existing users.
- Screens before first useful result: iOS about 12 to 13 prompts before Home (medium confidence; newest capture 2022); web about 6 with no personalization step (medium-high confidence, March 2024).
- Company statement: none justifying the three-artist minimum; the 2026 Taste Profile material frames personalization as deepening with engagement. No metrics.
- Sources: teardown, [Spotify onboarding on iOS, Pageflows, June 2022](https://pageflows.com/post/ios/onboarding/spotify/); teardown, [Spotify onboarding on web, Pageflows, March 2024](https://pageflows.com/post/desktop-web/onboarding/spotify/); teardown (secondhand, 403), [How Spotify Onboards New Users, Medium](https://medium.com/@TheAhmadkabir/how-spotify-onboards-new-users-and-what-id-improve-as-a-pm-c05b4eb318df); company statement, [Shape Your Taste Profile, Spotify Newsroom, 2026-03-13](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-03-13/taste-profile-beta-announcement/); user reports, [Spotify Community, Accounts](https://community.spotify.com/t5/Accounts/); company statement, [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spotify/id324684580), retrieved 2026-10-01.
- Gaps: both Medium teardowns 403; no Android flow; no 2026 screenshot confirming the 2022 iOS structure; the minimum reported as 3 or "3 or more".

## TikTok

- Flow, pre-login: (1) splash; (2) an interest-category screen, a multi-select grid of named categories (pets, travel, fashion, food, comedy), offered before any account exists; (3) if skipped, a generalized popular feed; (4) the For You feed plays at once, full screen; (5) browsing continues with no login wall; a sign-up prompt appears only on an identity-bound action (like, comment, follow, post, save). Flow at sign-up (Pageflows capture October 2022, archive back to November 2018): method (phone, email, username, or social); birthday; a CAPTCHA; password twice; permission requests (contacts, tracking, camera, microphone); "Find Facebook friends"; guidance overlays; push-notification permission; back to the feed logged in.
- Format: the interest picker is a multi-select tile grid of named categories, not a search box or sliders.
- Minimum items required: none found; the company's framing implies zero is acceptable.
- Skippable: the interest picker is fully skippable per TikTok's own statement; birthday appears mandatory at sign-up (inferred, medium confidence, likely age compliance).
- Imports: contacts access and "Find Facebook friends" inside the sign-up wizard only, not at the pre-login stage.
- Asked later: the interest screen can be revisited from settings to reset recommendations; push permission arrives late in sign-up; the App Store listing says "Your experience on TikTok is built around what you're into, not who you know", positioning implicit behavior as the engine and the picker as a cold-start nicety.
- Screens before first useful result: as few as 1 to 2 (splash, then an optional one-tap-to-skip picker) before a full-screen playing feed, because no account is required; medium-high confidence on the pattern, lower on the exact 2026 count.
- Company statement: the newsroom frames interest selection as something offered to help kick things off, with a no-penalty fallback to a popular feed (TikTok Newsroom, 2020-06-18). No metrics.
- Sources: company statement, [How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou, TikTok Newsroom, 2020-06-18](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you); company statement, [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiktok-videos-shop-live/id835599320), retrieved 2026-10-01; teardown, [TikTok onboarding on iOS, Pageflows, October 2022](https://pageflows.com/post/ios/onboarding/tiktok/); teardown, [TikTok's activation-focused onboarding, Appcues](https://goodux.appcues.com/blog/tiktok-user-onboarding), undated; how-to, [How to Change Your Interests on TikTok, SocialBoosting](https://www.socialboosting.com/blog/how-to-change-your-interests-on-tiktok), undated.
- Gaps: TikTok's current help pages unreadable; Mobbin 403; no exact interest count; no confirmation the 2018 to 2022 sign-up steps are unchanged in 2026.

## Cross-app notes

TikTok hands an anonymous user a populated, playing feed in one or two taps, while Netflix and Spotify gate everything behind full account creation before any personalization step. Netflix and TikTok use the same cold-start rhetoric in their own copy: skippers get a popular default, and real behavior supersedes the picker. Where a picker exists, tile-tap on a pre-populated grid is the dominant pattern, with Spotify layering a search box on top; neither leads with a free-text "name three things" search, a counterpoint to a favorites-by-search first step. Import and social-graph placement varies with no single pattern. Spotify's own web sign-up skips its artist picker entirely, so even Spotify treats the picker as a lever for the platform where engagement matters, not a universal requirement.
