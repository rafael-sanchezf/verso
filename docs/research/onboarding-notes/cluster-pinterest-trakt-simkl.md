# Onboarding flows: Pinterest, Trakt, Simkl (as of 2026-10-01)

Raw note from a research sub-agent of the first (stalled) inventory run on 2026-10-01, kept because its findings are dated and sourced. Method caveats from the agent: its web search budget ran out partway; reddit.com and web.archive.org failed at the tool level for every URL, so no forum reports or archived snapshots are included; no accounts were created.

## Pinterest

- Flow (the classic interest-tile onboarding, per Page Flows' iOS catalog): welcome; sign up (email, Google, or Facebook); password; age; notifications (OS prompt); gender; country; interests across two screens; a "building your Pinterest" loading state; the App Tracking Transparency prompt (two screens); Home with tooltips.
- Format: text and numeric fields for credentials and age; gender as a tappable option list; country as a dropdown; interests as a paginated tile grid of labeled images, tap to select, not a search box or a swipe deck.
- Minimum items required: five topics, reported consistently across secondary sources (UX Planet and growth blogs via search synthesis); not confirmed on a 2026 Pinterest page.
- Skippable: the interest step appears to be a hard gate per teardowns; age, gender, and country read as required; the OS prompts are dismissible by definition.
- Imports: none beyond Google or Facebook as an authentication shortcut.
- Asked later: Pinterest's Help Center describes an ongoing "Refine your recommendations" panel (activity, boards, interests, following, AI-content toggles); a 2026 release note (via search synthesis) adds a control to dial generative-AI content up or down. The five-topic pick is the seed; saves, searches, and browsing do the rest.
- Screens before first useful result: about 8 to 11 decision screens, 18 to 21 counting every sub-screen (Page Flows: about 21 screens, about 2 minutes 10 seconds; Mobbin: 18 for iOS, 14 for web). Confidence medium.
- Company statements, reported but unverified (both sources 403'd and the archive was unreachable): Casey Winters, Pinterest's former head of growth, on onboarding copy that became benefit-driven ("What topics do you care about? We'll show you the best content in those topics") and on reaching perceived value fast; a Pinterest Engineering post on localized onboarding (French, German, Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish) reporting users 5 to 10 percent more likely to return.
- Sources: teardown, [Pinterest iOS, Page Flows](https://pageflows.com/post/ios/onboarding/pinterest/), accessed 2026-10-01; teardown, [Pinterest iOS onboarding flow, Mobbin (metadata only)](https://mobbin.com/explore/flows/2925f408-9a22-43bc-a279-617e7cd35d80); teardown, [Pinterest onboarding, AppFuel](https://theappfuel.com/examples/pinterest_onboarding); teardown (403, search synthesis), [Pinterest's Personalization Playbook, UX Planet](https://uxplanet.org/pinterests-personalization-playbook-e677dfd1a155); company statement (403, search synthesis), [Casey Winters on Pinterest onboarding, Appcues on Medium](https://medium.com/appcues/casey-winters-reveals-how-pinterest-perfected-user-onboarding-639fcc7486d7); company statement (403, search synthesis), [Personalizing Pinterest's new user experience abroad, Pinterest Engineering](https://medium.com/pinterest-engineering/personalizing-pinterests-new-user-experience-abroad-60f8f55177ac); company statement, [Refine your recommendations, Pinterest Help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/tune-your-home-feed), accessed 2026-10-01; company statement, [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pinterest/id429047995), accessed 2026-10-01.
- Gaps: no 2026 Pinterest-owned confirmation of the five-topic minimum or the exact order (the flow has been A/B tested over the years); no Reddit; the two rationale sources unverified.

## Trakt

- Flow, two pictures the agent could not reconcile without signing up. (A) Trakt's official forum tutorial: choose an auth method (Google, Apple, or email with username and password); authenticate; confirm the email; a welcome screen offering to "import your data from other services"; the account is live, with profile, privacy, spoiler, and tracking-mode preferences in Settings. (B) Several third-party guides (one dated 2026-01-02): join; accept terms; profile info (display name, location, time format, gender, birthdate); country and streaming services; favorite genres for movies and TV; add shows and movies as watched, collected, watchlisted, or rated; optional social connections; optional push notifications; "Continue to Dashboard".
- Format: text fields and dropdowns; a terms checkbox; streaming services as a multi-select list; genres described only as "select"; content adding is search-driven (type a title, then mark watched, collect, watchlist, or rate 1 to 10), not a pre-populated deck.
- Minimum items required: none stated anywhere.
- Skippable: email confirmation is the one clearly required step; whether the wizard of picture B has a Skip, or is simply optional and reachable in Settings as the official tutorial implies, is unresolved.
- Imports, early and prominent: the TV Time importer (launched 2024-11-21) is available during sign-up for new users and in Settings for existing users (AlternativeTo); an IMDb and Letterboxd importer (2024-11-18) is positioned for initial setup; the Streaming Scrobbler (Netflix, Prime Video, Hulu, Apple TV+; 2024-12-05) syncs about ten minutes after linking and then every 24 hours, with no confirmation whether it is surfaced during onboarding.
- Asked later: per the official tutorial, username, email, birthday, and location edits, the public or private toggle, spoiler preferences, and single versus multiple watch tracking are Settings-only; the App Store listing frames ranking your favorite services as a settings action; the VIP tier surfaces once a user is active.
- Screens before first useful result: 3 to 4 on the minimal path to an empty dashboard; 9 to 13 if the full wizard is completed. Confidence low to medium.
- Company statement: none on onboarding design. No metrics.
- Sources: teardown, [Trakt TV on Kodi and streaming apps, TroyPoint, 2026-01-02](https://troypoint.com/trakt/); teardown, [What is Trakt TV, ReviewVPN, about 2023](https://reviewvpn.com/what-is-trakt-tv/); company statement, [How do I create an account at Trakt, Trakt Forums](https://forums.trakt.tv/t/how-do-i-create-an-account-at-trakt/112022), accessed 2026-10-01; press, [Trakt presents new TV Time data importer, AlternativeTo, 2024-11-21](https://alternativeto.net/news/2024/11/trakt-presents-new-tv-time-data-importer); press, [Trakt introduces importer for IMDb and Letterboxd, AlternativeTo, 2024-11-18](https://alternativeto.net/news/2024/11/trakt-introduces-importer-for-imdb-and-letterboxd); press, [Trakt launches Streaming Scrobbler, AlternativeTo, 2024-12-05](https://alternativeto.net/news/2024/12/trakt-tv-launches-streaming-scrobbler-to-sync-viewing-history-across-streaming-platforms/); company statement, [App Store listing v3.11](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trakt-tv-time-to-watch/id1514873602), accessed 2026-10-01.
- Gaps: whether the genre and profile wizard is shown to every new web sign-up or is a legacy flow; no minimum or Skip confirmation; no Reddit; the scrobbler's onboarding placement.

## Simkl

- Flow, per Simkl's documentation: create an account at simkl.com (email, Google, or Apple); verify the email (implied); land on a profile or dashboard. Everything else is optional and not sequenced: profile and timezone preferences in Settings, a one-click quick start at simkl.com/profile/create to mark popular, already-seen shows and movies as watched, an optional import of watch history (about fourteen sources), and optional connections to media centers (Kodi, Plex, VLC). No taste or genre questionnaire anywhere in the documentation.
- Format: text fields or OAuth buttons; the quick start is a tile grid of popular title artwork, tap to mark watched; imports as a list of source buttons; media centers as named integrations with a connect action.
- Minimum items required: none.
- Skippable: account creation (and verification) is the only required step; everything else is self-directed.
- Imports: offered as discoverable options ("Import Watch History (Optional)"), reachable from a documented link, not a wizard screen.
- Asked later: profile and timezone after login; Recommendations generated from accumulated history and ratings, with PRO and VIP accounts getting lists "automatically generated".
- Screens before first useful result: about 2 to an empty but functional dashboard; 3 to 4 with the quick-start tiles. Confidence medium; no independent teardown or user reports were found.
- Company statement: product claims for importing and connecting (a unified library, background sync, "AI-powered Discover and Smart Search", a permanent backup), none tied to onboarding-step design. No metrics.
- Sources: company statements, Simkl docs, accessed 2026-10-01: [Quick Start Guide](https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/getting-started-with-simkl/quick-start-guide), [Creating a Simkl Account](https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/getting-started-with-simkl/account-creation/creating-a-simkl-account), [What is Simkl](https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl), [Who is Simkl for](https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/about/what-is-simkl/who-is-simkl-for), [Recommendations](https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/core-features/search-and-discovery/recommendations), [Why Should You Import To SIMKL](https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/advanced-usage/import-export-data/importing-to-simkl/other-import-method/why-should-you-import-to-simkl); company statement, [App Store listing v1.6.1](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simkl-lists-tv-anime-movies/id1229691035), accessed 2026-10-01.
- Gaps: whether docs.simkl.org is run by Simkl Inc. or an affiliated team is unverified; simkl.com/profile/create returned 403, so the tile grid is secondhand; no Reddit; no independent coverage of the actual sign-up screens.

## Cross-app notes

Pinterest and the two trackers sit at opposite ends of a front-load-taste versus defer-everything spectrum, with Trakt in an ambiguous middle. Pinterest treats interest selection as a hard-gated, five-item, tile-tap activation step right after credentials, with a reported company belief that it improves return rates, and offers no import. Simkl is the cleanest minimal, utility-only onboarding: no taste step at all, imports and connections as optional conveniences, personalization built silently from logged activity and gated further behind a paid tier. Trakt may ask a favorite-genres question at sign-up, with low confidence on whether it is mandatory, and it is the one tracker that surfaces imports during sign-up. All three push deeper personalization well after sign-up, which supports deferring anything beyond a minimal taste signal.
