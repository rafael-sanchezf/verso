# Onboarding flows: Hardcover, Fable, Likewise (as of 2026-10-01)

Raw note from a research sub-agent of the first (stalled) inventory run on 2026-10-01, kept because its findings are dated and sourced. Method caveat from the agent: its web search budget ran out after about seventeen searches and the rest came from fetching pages already surfaced.

## Hardcover

- Flow: (1) the app is browsable in a limited form before login; (2) Join; (3) pick a profile photo; (4) choose a username; (5) choose to import a library from Goodreads, The StoryGraph, or a custom CSV, or skip; (6) set a reading goal; (7) follow other users; (8) optionally add more profile details.
- Format: the import is a three-way choice of file uploads, not an account link. No genre chips and no "name your favorites" step exist anywhere in Hardcover's onboarding in any source; personalization comes from the import or from the library and follows built by hand.
- Minimum items required: none found.
- Skippable: the final details step is explicitly optional; the import is presented as a choice (optional by implication); no source confirms a visible Skip on the goal or follow steps.
- Imports: Goodreads, StoryGraph, and generic CSV during onboarding, right after the username (teardown, BookWise, 2026-03-23: "supports imports from: Goodreads, StoryGraph, Italic Type, Custom CSV format"; teardown, House Lucia, 2026-05-08). No social sign-in details found.
- Asked later: not documented. App Store release notes (2025-12-19 v2.1.2; 2026-06-19 v2.1.3) describe a new dashboard and a swipe-back gesture only.
- Screens before first useful result: about 5 to 7 to reach a populated library through import; without an import the user starts empty. Confidence moderate (one consistent teardown).
- Company rationale: none found; hardcover.app/pages/support returned 403. No metrics; a small indie app (Android rating 3.83 to 4.2 on roughly 66 to 150 ratings).
- Sources: teardown, [13 book tracking apps reviewed, House Lucia, 2026-05-08](https://houselucia.com/book-tracking-apps-2026/); teardown, [The Hardcover App Review 2026, BookWise, 2026-03-23](https://bookwiseapp.com/blog/hardcover-app); teardown, [Eight Thoughts on the Hardcover App, 2026-09-08](https://readbakecreate.com/eight-thoughts-on-the-hardcover-app/); company statement, [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hardcover-app/id1663379893), fetched 2026-10-01; user reports, [justuseapp reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1663379893/hardcover-app/reviews); teardown, [10 Best Goodreads Alternatives, 2026-03-31](https://bestwriting.com/goodreads-alternatives).
- Gaps: no primary confirmation of skip controls, minimums, or later prompts; no 2025 to 2026 Reddit walkthrough.

## Fable

- Status: acquired by Everand (Scribd), closing 2025-06-09 (company statement, BusinessWire). Fable still runs as its own app in 2026; on 2026-06-02 Everand and Fable launched a shared subscription with progress and saved titles syncing from Everand into Fable, and Fable's own account system and onboarding stay distinct (Publishers Weekly and PR Newswire, 2026-06-02; support.scribd.com, current).
- Flow, two eras: historically (2022 to 2023 reviews) no structured onboarding, sign up and land in the feed, with the Goodreads import under Profile, Settings afterwards. On 2026-07-22 and 2026-07-25 Fable shipped App Store updates labeled "Improved onboarding experience" (v277, v278); the new flow's screens could not be retrieved. Fable's help center documents a standing content-preferences flow: choose Books, TV Shows, or Both, then optionally pick genre tags.
- Format: genre tags as tap tiles ("select what types of book genres and/or TV show genres you usually like"), reachable at Profile, Settings, Manage content preferences. No "name three favorites" or swipe deck found. Sign-in is email and password; Microsoft SSO is being retired; Google or Apple sign-in unconfirmed.
- Minimum items required: none; the help article says preferences save even with zero genres picked.
- Skippable: genre selection confirmed skippable; whether the Books, TV, or Both choice can be skipped is unconfirmed.
- Imports: Goodreads import (including CSV) documented only as a post-sign-up Settings action (help.fable.co articles 128 and 131). Everand and Fable account linking is done from Everand's settings, for paying Everand plans only, and does not touch reading lists or history (support.scribd.com). A contacts-import feature exists (a release note around 2026-09-26 fixes it); whether it is offered during onboarding is unconfirmed.
- Asked later: content-preference tags editable in Settings, the clearest "ask less at sign-up, refine later" example. Fable's "Scout" AI recommendations and a "mood quiz" (a TikTok hit, unverified) appear to be in-app features, not sign-up questions.
- Screens before first useful result: low confidence and conflicting: pre-July-2026 reviews imply 0 to 1; an Appllama teardown catalogs "Join or Sign In" plus thirteen onboarding screens, paywalled and undated. Best estimate 2 to 6 as of 2026-10-01.
- Company rationale: only the release-note label "Improved onboarding experience". No metrics.
- Sources: company statement, [BusinessWire, 2025-06-09](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250609165978/en/); company statement, [PR Newswire, 2026-06-02](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/everand-and-fable-launch-a-single-shared-subscription-302788169.html); company statement, [Everand and Fable: What You Need to Know](https://support.scribd.com/hc/en-us/articles/38205474518164) and [Linking my Everand and Fable Accounts](https://support.scribd.com/hc/en-us/articles/47886300785300); company statement, [How do I change my TV Show and Books content preferences, help.fable.co article 115](https://help.fable.co/article/115-how-do-i-change-my-tv-show-and-books-content-preferences); company statement, help.fable.co articles 128 and 131 on the Goodreads import and [article 169, Microsoft Sign-In](https://help.fable.co/article/169-microsoft-sign-in); company statement, [App Store release notes v277, v278, v285](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fable-track-discuss-books/id1488170618); teardown, [House Lucia, 2026-05-08](https://houselucia.com/book-tracking-apps-2026/); teardown, [Appllama screens, paywalled](https://appllama.io/apps/1488170618/fable-track-discuss-books); user report, [BookRiot, 2022-07-20](https://bookriot.com/fable-book-club-app-review/).
- Gaps: the post-July-2026 onboarding sequence is unknown, the biggest gap; social sign-in unconfirmed; contacts-import timing unconfirmed.

## Likewise

- Status: majority acquired by Watchlist Inc. on 2025-06-04 (PR Newswire, confirmed by direct fetch). The consumer app is live and updated (App Store v32.38 about 2026-09-28; Google Play last updated 2026-08-31 per a snippet). likewise.com now redirects to pix-media.com (observed 2026-10-01).
- Flow (2021 to 2023 baseline): (1) account creation with email, password, and optional demographics (birthday, gender, phone); (2) choose content types, Books, TV and Movies, Podcasts, any combination; (3) per category, broad genre chips with conditional sub-genre chips (comedy reveals dark comedy, teen comedy); (4) select specific titles from a pre-populated, genre-informed grid, with a search bar to add titles not shown; (5) optionally follow suggested users.
- Format: a hybrid: chips for genres, then tile taps plus a free-text search for titles (one user report describes using the search box repeatedly to add an author's books).
- Minimum items required: 10 books, 20 movies and TV titles, 5 podcasts per chosen category before the app proceeds (teardown, MakeUseOf, 2021-07-06; the 10-book figure corroborated by a 2023 review). Not re-confirmed against the 2026 build.
- Skippable: historically account creation was not skippable (a user report: "I would never keep an app that demanded I give it my name, email or other personal information before it even allowed me to look at Likewise"). By about 2026-09-28 the App Store notes advertise "Try the app without signing up! Explore reviews, discussions, asks, and the latest trending lists": browsing without an account, with the survey presumably still gating personalization. Whether the 10/20/5 minimums still apply on sign-up is unconfirmed.
- Imports: none found; the onboarding is a self-contained taste survey.
- Asked later: poorly documented; one user could not get back to the other categories after the first pass.
- Screens before first useful result: about 5 to 6 under the historical flow; near 0 for browsing without an account since September 2026.
- Company statement: CEO Ian Morris: "Nearly 90% of Likewise users provide some information on their media preferences on signup... and more than 80% provide demographic data, as well" (AdExchanger, 2023-12-11), which implies both sections are optional; a 2023 first-person review places the demographic fields right after email and password, before any taste content (BookRiot, 2023-03-30).
- Sources: company statement, [PR Newswire, 2025-06-04](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/likewise-announces-acquisition-by-watchlist-inc-betting-big-on-the-future-of-newsletters-and-personalized-entertainment-discovery-302473317.html); teardown, [MakeUseOf, 2021-07-06](https://www.makeuseof.com/what-is-likewise/); teardown, [How-To Geek, updated 2021-09-10](https://www.howtogeek.com/48156/icydk-likewise-app-offers-actually-good-recommendations/); user report, [BookRiot, 2023-03-30](https://bookriot.com/likewise-app-review-for-books/); company statement, [AdExchanger, 2023-12-11](https://www.adexchanger.com/publishers/why-likewise-is-focusing-on-direct-deals-instead-of-programmatic/); company statement, [App Store listing v32.38](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/likewise-movie-tv-book-recs/id1264195462), fetched 2026-10-01; user reports, [justuseapp reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1264195462/likewise-movie-tv-book-recs/reviews).
- Gaps: no 2026 walkthrough confirming the minimums or the new browse-first path; Google Play not re-fetched; no official post on the change.

## Cross-app notes

Hardcover has no taste quiz at all; its bet is the import, placed at the front, with no minimums. Likewise is the closest analogue to Verso's deck and pushes further: hard minimums (10/20/5), a search box for specific titles on top of genre chips and a tile grid, and full personalization gated behind all of it, until it added a browse-without-signing-up path in September 2026, a sign the gate was too heavy for browsers. Fable ran for years with no onboarding, dropping users into the feed, and shipped an "improved onboarding experience" in July 2026, which suggests a zero-quiz cold start was judged insufficient, though nothing says what replaced it. Both book apps treat imports as a Settings afterthought (Fable explicitly; Everand linking for paying subscribers only), a data point against surfacing imports during onboarding, against Hardcover's opposite choice. Likewise's demographics-before-taste order is the one concrete example of that sequence, and a pattern to avoid.
