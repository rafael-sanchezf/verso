# Verso usability test plan

## Purpose

Find out whether someone who has never seen Verso can complete the core loop, logging a finished game and finding one to play next, without help. This tests the words and the flow, not the visuals; the frames are structure, not final polish.

## Setup

Open the Verso Figma file, Structure round page, row eight (the newest frames: Game page B v2, the tier sheet that expands into comparisons, Next v3, Home long-press v2, Search add-to sheet v2). Ranking A v2 sits a row or two earlier and hasn't changed since. Use Presentation mode and tap through frames like a working app, or just point at the right frame on the canvas if the links misbehave. One friend at a time, about fifteen minutes each.

Say: "This is an early design for an app called Verso. I'll read you a task. Talk out loud, tell me what you're looking at and what you expect before you tap." Don't say what a button does, where to tap, or "yes, that's right" mid-task. If they stall, ask "what are you looking at right now?" instead of pointing. Rescue them only once they're fully stuck, then move on.

## Tasks

One task per friend. Read the wording exactly as written.

**Friend 1** — Say: "Log a game you just finished and place it in your ranking."
Starts on: Home, the Playing block.
Expected path: tap the game in Playing → game page → "I finished this" → pick a tier → sheet expands into a comparison → pick a winner → confirm placement.
Success: reaches confirm placement and taps it, unassisted.

**Friend 2** — Say: "Find something to play tonight from your backlog."
Starts on: Home.
Expected path: Home's "Up next" block or the Next tab → Backlog → settles on one game.
Success: points to or taps a specific game and can say why.

**Friend 3** — Say: "Mark a game you own on two platforms and add it to your backlog."
Starts on: Search.
Expected path: search for a game → the add-to sheet → mark two platforms owned → add to backlog.
Success: two platforms show owned and the game lands in Backlog, with no "what does owned mean" question.

## Trunk test

Show any single frame and ask just these five, in order:

1. What app is this?
2. What medium am I in?
3. Where am I?
4. How do I get back?
5. How do I add a game?

## What to record

For each friend, note:

| Where they hesitated | What they tapped first | Words they misread | Anything they said was missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

Copy this table three times, once per friend.

## Fix only what two of three people stumbled on

One person's confusion might just be that person. Two means it's the design.
